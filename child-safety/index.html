Child Safety Standards

Last updated 13 October 2026

Be Alert has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking. These standards apply to everything on Be Alert — posts, photos, comments, profiles and business listings.

Be Alert is offered on the website bealert.online, as a web app at app.bealert.online and as an Android app. They are one service with one account, and this document applies to all of them.

What is not allowed

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children, in any form, including drawn, edited or AI-generated content.
  • Contacting, grooming or soliciting a child, or arranging to meet a child for any harmful purpose.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material.
  • Using lost & found, jobs, services or any other section to target, track or exploit children.

Who can use Be Alert

Be Alert is meant for adults. Sellers and service providers must verify their CNIC and be approved by our team before they can post in those sections.

How to report

  • In the app: tap “Report” on any post. Every report is reviewed by the Be Alert team, and posts with several reports are hidden until reviewed. To report a comment or a profile, email us.
  • By email: razachaudhary5512@gmail.com — our child safety contact.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call the police (15) or Rescue 1122 first.

What we do

  • Remove violating content as soon as we become aware of it and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • Preserve the relevant records and report CSAM and child exploitation to the authorities, including the FIA Cyber Crime Wing in Pakistan and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Cooperate with law enforcement requests in line with applicable law.

Compliance

Be Alert complies with the child safety laws that apply to it and with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy.
