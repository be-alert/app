Delete your Be Alert account

Last updated 13 October 2026

You can permanently delete your Be Alert account and the data linked to it at any time, from the Android app or from this page. Deletion happens straight away — there is no waiting period.

How to delete your account

  • In the Android app or web app: open Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.
  • On this page: sign in below and press “Delete my account”.
  • Forgot your password? Tap “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen — we’ll email you a code to set a new one. If you joined with Google, just use “Continue with Google”.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address, display name, profile photo and settings.
  • Your posts, photos, comments, votes, RSVPs, bookmarks, reports, trusted contacts and business listings.
  • Your CNIC and driving-licence details, and any verification photos still waiting for review.
  • If you signed in with Google, the name and email we received from Google.

What is kept

Records of moderation actions taken by Be Alert admins (for example, that a post was removed) are kept for safety, without your name, email or other personal details. Nothing else is kept.

Questions or requests: use “Report” on any post, or contact the Be Alert team through the app. Website: bealert.online
